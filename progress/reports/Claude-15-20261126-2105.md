## Conclusion

1. **New base:** `Inditas-Csomag.cmd` option `1` = 512 px + PC FSR + `VICTORY_TEXTURE_LOOK_MS=250`.
   - Option `2` is the same with a frame strip; `3` and `4` are spares.
   - `KEEP_SHOWN=2` left the menu.
   - `meres-csomag.ps1`'s `-KeepShown` parameter stays.
2. **`KEEP_SHOWN=2` did not reduce the pop-in,** even though it kept the old texture in thousands of replacements of another kind. So the mechanism suspected in Claude-14 (a slot reading zeros) is at most a small part of the fault seen. It may also be that the kept old mask of another shape itself shows as pop-in.
3. **Codex's case 1 stays open:** the whole object vanishes and returns at the level of the draw or the LOD switch. That needs following the tree's draw command and instance count before and after the flash.
   - The bundle's log does not give this per object.
   - According to the README, recording a frame in the game world sets off the game's own watchdog.
   - This needs a measuring point of its own.
