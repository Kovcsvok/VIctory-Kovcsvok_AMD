**Conclusions:**

2. **Slow model loading:** according to avatarmanz's README the game paces its loading to its own frames, so at 10 fps it loads about three times slower than at 30. At 15–20 fps models stay longer at the lowest detail. The 60 fps pacing probably makes it worse too, because the game plans its work for a 16.7 ms frame while 50–60 ms frames come. The two effects cannot be separated in this run.

## 2. Changes

**`meres-csomag.ps1`:**
- `-FaJavitas ki|be`, default **`ki`** (`VICTORY_PORT_CONSTANTS_ORDER=0`). The fix only turns on when asked.
- New `-Port1 be|ki` parameter. `ki` = `VICTORY_PORT_CONSTANTS=0`; ports 2, 5, 6 and 7 stay.

**Why can port 1 be turned off on its own?**
- Port 1 only affects the host side (`port.rs`). Port 5 changes shader compilation (`victory_shader::emit::shader::constants_raw`), so nothing needs recompiling, and the shader cache stays valid.
- Port 5's in-place reads (`draw.rs`, `port_cbv::in_place_on`) only use port 1's in-place copies. Without them it switches to copying (`port_cbv::copied(true)`).
- **Test start** without port 1 (100 s, menu): no panic, no ERROR. 29.5–31.2 fps, submission preparation 22 ms, 1,400–1,900 draws.

Every option's dry run was fine.

**Why this tree test?** In avatarmanz's pop-10 run the trees did not flicker without ports 1 and 5 (`draw.rs`, the comment on `in_place_order`). If the tree line does not drop out in the option-`6` run, port 1 causes the flicker here too. Then the choice is: play without the port if 30 fps holds, or look for a cheaper ordering fix. If the trees still flicker, the trail leads to port 5 or elsewhere.

## 3. Checking the video: the tree flickers even with the fix

I went through all 451 frames of the 41–56 s stretch. After dense extraction I used the same flash finder as in Claude-18. In this stretch the boat runs ashore and the camera stands among trees.

- **54.67 s:** a whole tree on the right **appears** for a single frame; it is not there before or after (crop `Fajav-be-54s67-fa-felvillan.jpg`, local only).
- **55.57 s:** the same tree, visible at 55.53 and 55.60 s, **vanishes** for one frame (crop `Fajav-be-55s57-fa-kiesik.jpg`, local only).
- The same block group also fires at 54.37, 54.67, 55.27 and 55.57 s, so the tree flickers in and out several times.

**fix/popin turned on** (by the log with 1,273 single executions a frame) **does not remove** the flicker of whole objects. So either something else is involved in our fault than in avatarmanz's pop-5…13 runs, or the fix does not cover it. The option-`6` tree test (without port 1) tells them apart.
