# Claude-34 – The RAM overflow in long play: buffer views of every size filled the view table

Date: 2026-11-27, 18:50–21:55 (local time). Tree: `VIctory-dev\belobug-9070` (uncommitted, base cd8dfc06). Follows Claude-33. The user played on for 36 minutes. RAM filled slowly until the game ran at 4 fps: private 35.9 GB, the PC's RAM 29.0 of 31.8 GB.

## 1. What filled the RAM (run `20261127-181452-csomag-alap`, 41.6 min, the user's own play)

The run's `victory.log` was written when the game closed (67 MB). Private memory grew by about 0.4 GB a minute after the load, from 23.7 GB to 36.7 GB. Card memory, shared GPU memory, host lists, window copies (2.8–4.1 GB) and host textures stayed flat. Our own allocations grew:

| Time into the run | Shapes of placed views | View table entries | View table, MB | Our own allocations, MB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 min | 1,473,281 | 8,381 | 480 | 2,145 |
| 10 min | 3,070,069 | 8,515 | 977 | 2,815 |
| 15 min | 4,194,304 (full) | 1,283,625 | 1,682 | 3,620 |
| 20 min | full | 6,498,452 | 3,512 | 5,545 |
| 36 min (the screenshot) | full | 17,645,890 | 8,945 | 11,106 |
| 41 min | full | 20,260,994 | 9,454 | 11,619 |

- `descriptor.rs` names a view of placed memory in its 8-byte record by its address and a shape number. The shape is everything but the address, and it was kept for the run.
- The design assumed as many shapes as the title has kinds of textures and buffers. When the 4,194,304 shape numbers ran out, every new view became a table entry, and nothing frees those.
- The 10-minute runs of Claude-31–33 had the same growth (2.86 million shapes, 0.9 GB at 10 min) but ended before the table was full.

## 2. Why: buffer views of ever other sizes (run `20261127-185642-alak-cenzus`, 6 min, census)

- A diagnostics census (`descriptor.rs` `shape_made`, line `memory, shapes of placed views`) compared each new shape with the last new one of its kind.
- Of 1,351,548 new shapes, 1,346,742 were buffers (99.6 %) and 4,806 textures.
- The words that differ:
  - the resource's width (r4): 96 %;
  - the view's count of elements (v6): 94 %;
  - the structure stride (v7): 38 %;
  - the words the title leaves its stack in (r1, r13, v3, v9): 17–33 %.
- In the logged samples the width is the count times the stride (`0xf3c00` = 2,400 × 416), or that rounded up to 16 bytes for raw views.
- The title writes about 200,000 buffer views a second, of transient buffers of every size.

## 3. Fix (`VICTORY_VIEW_BUFFERS`, on by default)

**New record kind `PLACED_BUFFER` (3).** A buffer view of placed memory that starts at its first element, and whose width follows from its count, is named whole. Its 62 bits hold the number of a buffer shape (8 bits), the count, the address and a layout bit:

| Layout | Address alignment | Count bits | Address bits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 16 bytes | 17 | 36 |
| 1 | 4 bytes | 15 | 38 |

- **Buffer shapes:** the description without count, width, address and filler. At most 256; 74–101 were used in play.
- **Reading back:** the record is decoded with the width rebuilt from the count (`Views::placed_buffer_of`). The host's 64-bit key still names one view.
- **Filler:** the words the title leaves its stack in (resource words 1 and 13, view words 3 and 9) are left out of buffer views. They are padding that D3D12 does not read, so views that differ only in them share one record.
- **Bounded fallback for views that do not fit:**
  - The old shapes stop at 131,072 (`SHAPES_MOST`).
  - Past 262,144 table entries (`VICTORY_VIEW_ENTRIES_MOST`), a new entry takes the entry of a view that carries an address (constant or placed) and was not written or read for 30 s. It takes it under a new generation, so records naming the old view go stale (null) and never name another view.
  - Samplers and views of resources are never given away.
- **Switching:** the switch changes live with `VICTORY_AB` (diagnostics build). Records of both ways read back whichever is on.
- **Log fields:** the census line adds buffer views named whole, buffer shapes, buffer views given a shape by reason, and entries given away.
- **Tests (3 new, `descriptor::tests`):**
  - `buffer_views_of_every_size_are_named_whole`: 60,000 structured, raw and unordered views of every count to 131,071, at 16- and 4-byte alignment. Each reads back exactly, with no shape and no entry: 4 buffer shapes. Filler variants share their record.
  - `buffer_views_that_do_not_fit_keep_their_shapes`: a first element, an odd width, an odd address and 200,000 elements. These keep the old path and read back.
  - `a_full_table_gives_idle_loose_entries_away`.
  - victory-gpu: 446 passed, 0 failed.

## 4. Antigravity's parallel fix (`agy-9070-v2` 353b50a, read only)

The user started Antigravity on the same problem while this work was under way. Its numbers for the 41-minute run match §1. It arrived at the same record layout.

**Taken from it:**
- The buffer-dimension check in `buffer_parts`.
- No buffer shape for a view that cannot fit anyway.
- A bounded fallback, with old shapes capped at 1 << 17.

**Not taken:**
- **Its entry ring** gives away any entry without a resource object, from the oldest on. Those include samplers, which are made at the start, so they would go first once the table fills, and sampler records would go stale. Ours gives away only constant and placed views that were not used for 30 s.
- **Secondary items:**
  - `Shadow::register` retire and a 2 GiB window budget: the windows were flat at 2.8–4.1 GB in the long run.
  - A cap of 64 on `thin::BACKS`: with `VICTORY_LIST_LAZY` the backs hold no host lists.
  - mimalloc: not in the offline registry, unmeasured.
  - A shorter `keep_freed` near the commit limit: this is a candidate for the RAM peak, not this leak.

## 5. Runs with the fix (target machine, the save loaded by `VICTORY_AUTO_A=25,100,5`, 10 min each, no device error)

**Run `20261127-212924-nezet-javitas` (fix on), against `175931-shader-egyszer` (before) at the same time into the run:**

| | Before (10 min) | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Shapes of placed views | 2,861,374 | 5,777 (+101 buffer shapes) |
| View table | 943 MB | 6 MB |
| Our own allocations | 2,754 MB | 1,778 MB |
| Their growth, minutes 4–9 | 130 MB/min | 20–25 MB/min |
| Private growth, minutes 4–9 | 0.26–0.32 GB/min | ~0.1 GB/min |

- 119 million buffer views were named whole.
- 645,152 did not fit (address or count) and reused the 674 old shapes of buffers.
- No table entry was given away.

**Run `20261127-214211-nezet-ab`, live A/B of `VICTORY_VIEW_BUFFERS` every 30 s, from the third minute:**

| | Off (old way, bounded) | On |
|---|---|---|
| Phases | 7 | 7 |
| Mean of phase means | 59.2 ms | **49.8 ms** |
| Median of medians | 51.7 ms | 45.6 ms |
| Mean p90 | 86.2 ms | **59.2 ms** |

- **The fix makes frames shorter, not longer.** Each "on" phase against the mean of its two neighbours: −5.5, −8.6, −14.0, −9.2, −12.4, −3.5 ms (mean −8.9).
- The smaller maps are cheaper to look up and lock.
- Both runs are slower overall than the 17:xx runs (42 ms), with more frames over 50 ms (28.8 % against 6.0 %). Antigravity and Codex were running from 21:17; the run guide asks for Antigravity to be closed. The A/B within one run removes that from the comparison.

## 6. What is left

- **A slower growth.** Private grew about 0.1 GB a minute in minutes 4–9, of which ~25 MB/min is our small blocks (<4 KiB, +90,000 blocks a minute) and the rest is window copies varying with the scene. Not identified yet: a 30–40-minute run would show whether it flattens. One small candidate seen: `resource.rs` `PLACED_RAW`, a set kept only for a log count, grows with every filler variant of a placed texture (+7,000 a minute).
- The RAM peak at the world load (window copies) and the late high-detail models (Claude-33 §5) are unchanged.
