## 2. The "textures stretching into infinity" are the missing boat fix

Evidence image: `meresek\LEARN-ki-nyulas-szakall.jpg` (local only).

**In the video:**
- **255 s (09:27:46 UTC = 10:27:46):** a boat stretches into a huge black wedge on the water next to Jason's boat.
- **164 s (10:26:15):** two long straight lines run from the seaplane's wing tips to the edge of the picture.

**Why the boat fix:**
- This is exactly what `CARD_STEPS_LEARN` (avatarmanz PR 434) fixes. The reason given in the code: the game rewrites its constant buffers after submitting, and the vertices of moving characters and of the boat "fly off".
- In the 09:32 run (LEARN on) I saw no stretching in the same boat chase.
- In the menu-9 live test the user noticed no stretching, but there the setting switched every 10 seconds and part of the cutscene was skipped.

## 3. The vanishing beard: the same cause

**In the video (same shots):**

| Time | Picture |
|---|---|
| 117–122 s | Jason has his beard |
| 124–127 s | **Cal's beard and moustache are missing** |
| 141–150 s | Cal's beard is there |
| 138–158 s | **Jason's beard is missing** (138, 139, 140, 152, 153), and does not return until the end of the shot |

**Compared with earlier runs:**
- **The 10:04 run** (the menu-9 live test): Cal had his beard in the same shot (149 s).
- **The 09:32 run** (LEARN on, the ray-tracing resolve skipped then too): Jason has his beard in the scene's last shot (12–13 s).

**Conclusion:**
- The beard is also a moving character mesh, with the same late constant buffers. Without LEARN its vertices fly off and the mesh disappears.
- **Not the skipped ray-tracing resolve:** it was skipped in the 09:32 run too, and the beard was there.
- **Not the texture queue:** long-waiting textures (the queue's oldest entry 4,667 and 7,241 frames) occurred in the 09:32 run (up to 13,404) and in the 10:04 run (up to 4,149) as well.

*(Later correction, Claude-28: with the card steps off the beard was gone too, so the more likely cause is lost writes into compressed textures.)*

## 5. The glasses: Antigravity's reflection clamp as a switch

**In the video:** the counsellor's glasses reflect the sky in white at 22, 24, 34 and 74 s, as in the 09:32 run. This is not the character ray-tracing resolve but the reflections' denoiser (`sr_denoising:CS_UpdateHistory`): without a BVH a ray hits no wall and returns the sky.

**Taken from `agy-9070-v2` `ebd4988`** (`crates/victory-gpu/src/ambient.rs`, `draw.rs`):
- `Role::Reflect`: the `hold_reflect` compute pass after `CS_UpdateHistory` clamps the reflection to 0.5 times the room's light (`VICTORY_AMBIENT_REFLECT`) and zeroes NaNs and magenta garbage;
- hooked in after the indirect dispatch, but only for this role.

**My own change:**
- new switch: `VICTORY_REFLECT_HOLD=1`, **off by default**, switchable with live A/B;
- I did not take Antigravity's other default changes: `AMBIENT_MOST` 2 instead of 4, NaN filtering in the air and in `raise`.

**The source:** Antigravity names the maren-halv commits `6c30d582` and `dd836294`. Neither is in any local repository, so **I could not compare against the original**.

**Checks:**
- build fine;
- the `ambient` tests 3/3 (including compiling the HLSL passes);
- a 75 s test start with `VICTORY_AB=10,VICTORY_REFLECT_HOLD`: the clamp ran (`a stand-in for the indirect light role=Reflect size=[1280, 720]`), which means the target really was R32_UINT (R9G9B9E5). No `no stand-in`, device loss or crash.
- **Not yet seen in a picture.**

**New build:** `victory.exe` `f61de3f0…`. Patch `csomag\csomag-belobug-9070.patch` `936873f0…`, reverse-checked. The shader cache key did not change; no need to pre-build again.
