# Claude-05 · B2 (large page file): no crash, but the stutters come from remapping memory

26 November 2026, 17:19 (local time). Previous: [Claude-04](Claude-04-20261126-1648.md).

## In short

1. **B2 did not crash.**
   - 13.1 minutes of running, 10.9 of them in the world, normal exit (exit code 0).
   - Free commit stayed above 25 GiB throughout (minimum 25.6 GiB). The page file became 40 GiB; the commit limit 71.85 GiB.
   - B1 stopped in its 9.3rd world minute; B2 ran 1.6 minutes longer.
   - After one run this is weak evidence that the stop was caused by commit running out, since A0b did not crash with little commit either. The page-file setting is worth keeping.
2. **The stutters did not improve.** With the same 512 px profile, 13.7% of the intervals in B2's first 10 minutes had a frame of at least 500 ms. In B1 it was 1.5%, in A0b 8.2%.
   - **I withdraw Claude-04's claim:** the stutter reduction of `-LowMemory` did not repeat. The spread between runs is bigger than the profile's effect.
3. **The logs show the mechanism of the stutters.**
   - The long stutters come in windows where the game spends most of its time in memory-remapping calls. These calls map the Xbox's physical memory onto Windows views (`victory-hle` `xmem.rs`).
   - Then single Windows mapping calls become slow: in B2 the p90 is 1.0 ms, the maximum 4.5 ms. The calls' median is about 10 µs.
   - DirectStorage reads slow down at the same time (slowest request 3.4 s).
   - All of this is independent of commit, since B2 had plenty of free commit.
4. **The code for the fix exists, and KLBF lacks it.**
   - cool_fox's commits `49d7126` and `565f4a5` describe and target exactly this bottleneck.
   - Both are in Victory United too; KLBF has neither.
   - They were made before `ab75ac3` and are independent of it.
5. **Picture faults (the user's screenshots, 17:09 and 17:13):**
   - The very dark shadows and the dark car belong to the known fault family of missing ambient light. That car windows are not drawn is also a known fault.
   - The red and cyan coloured edges are not on KLBF's list of known faults. They may be the game's own lens effect; two pictures cannot decide it.

## 2. What happens during a long stutter?

**The worst frame (17:11:03, 3,364 ms)** according to `frametime.csv`:
- CPU load was 5% instead of the usual 31%;
- our own preparation took 30 ms, texture uploads 4.5 ms.

So the translation layer was not working but waiting. `victory.log` at the same time:

- `dstorage: last 5.9 s … slowest_ms=2728 … arrays_waited_ms=1368`: the game's file reading waited 2.7 seconds for one request.
- `title memory remapped … 375 calls of the title, 1670.5 ms in them`: of 7 seconds, the game spent 1.7 remapping memory.
- `map with neither under way: 5914 calls, 490 us each`: a mapping call took half a millisecond on average.

**The relation is the same in all four runs.** By the `title memory remapped` log lines (~7 s windows), the share of the game's time spent remapping against the share of intervals containing a frame of at least 500 ms:

| The game's time spent remapping | A0 | A0b | B1 | B2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| < 5% | 0.4% | 1.1% | 0.8% | 0.8% |
| 5–15% | 1.5% | 1.8% | 4.3% | 4.0% |
| 15–30% | 5.7% | 6.7% | 3.7% | 20.7% |
| ≥ 30% | – | **28.5%** | – (did not occur) | **71.4%** |

- The ≥ 30% windows have fewer calls (median 272–372 in a window, 737–953 in the low band), so **it is not more calls coming, but every call being slower**.
- The Windows mapping calls' duration: in A0, A0b, B1 and B2 the p90 was 132, 648, 165 and 1,039 µs, the maximum 277, 1,205, 448 and 4,450 µs.
- B1 never got into this state, which is why it had few stutters. The profile played no part in it.

**Free RAM alone does not explain it.**
- In A0b the slow windows came with 0–2 GiB of free RAM.
- In B1 mapping did not slow down even at 0–2 GiB.
- In B2 it slowed down with 3–4 GiB free, with raised page-ins (3,500–7,000 pages a second, about 500 otherwise).
- In the slow windows the working set (22–24 GiB) and the shared GPU memory (8.5–9.4 GiB) were large in all three runs.

## 3. The missing code: cool_fox `49d7126` and `565f4a5`

The donor's code comments describe what we measured.

- **`49d7126`** (2026-11-25, "Streaming: mapping calls ask only for what they need; the thread that loads runs ahead"):
  - files: `xmem.rs` (248 lines) and `dstorage.rs` (119 lines), +345/−34 in all;
  - `VICTORY_MAP_LEAN`: mapping does not split a region that is already its own placeholder again, and does not commit pages that are already committed. By the comment, mapping a page takes 200–400 µs in the world and 10 µs in the menu, and up to four calls may be needed per page (unmap, split, map, commit), twice;
  - `VICTORY_STREAM_PRIORITY`: the game's below-normal-priority streaming thread is raised above normal for mapping and submitting.
- **`565f4a5`** (2026-11-25, "Pages are mapped side by side: eight threads stand in the host's queue instead of one"):
  - file: `xmem.rs` +460/−29;
  - `VICTORY_MAP_THREADS`: by default 8 threads map at once. By the comment, mapping and unmapping views wait on the same single address-space lock as the protection guards' roughly 100,000 protection changes a second, so a page takes 0.2–4 ms. In the author's measurement 1 thread mapped 1,500 pages a second, 8 threads 30,000;
  - tests: `map_bench` (line 2354) and `mapping_is_busy_from_a_quarter_of_the_time_to_a_twentieth` (line 2522).
- KLBF already has the counterpart of `9fea743`'s `VICTORY_SECTION_WINDOW` switch: `VICTORY_SECTION_VIEWS`; by the log every write goes through the permanent view.

**Places in the two trees:**

## 4. Picture faults

- **Dark shadows, a dark car, a white rear windscreen.** According to lines 58–65 of KLBF's `README.md`, car windows are not drawn and mirrors are black, because they need ray tracing. The earlier labs' changelog also says several times that the picture stayed dark (`CHANGELOG.md` 32 and 41). The fix candidate is item 6 of the fork notes: Belobug's ambient-light stand-in. That is a separate picture-quality branch, after the stutters.
- **Red/cyan coloured edges on high-contrast edges.** Not among KLBF's known faults. It may be the game's own lens effect (chromatic aberration), or an upscaling fault. Deciding needs a screenshot in a menu or indoors, without motion.
- **Flicker:** the user did not find it unusual in this run.
