## 2. The upstream fix: avatarmanz `fix/popin` (7ab13ed)

Antigravity pointed it out, I checked it. In `donor-avatarmanz-VIctory-7900XTX`'s `main`, fetched today, 7ab13ed says:

> Merge fix/popin: indirect draws whose arguments sit in port 1's in-place memory are prepared one at a time right before they run (VICTORY_PORT_CONSTANTS_ORDER, on by default): trees and palms no longer drop out, lamp-lit rooms steady (runs pop-14..17)

**What happened, according to the code comments** (`draw.rs`, `in_place_order` and `in_place_alone`):
- Renderer port 1 (`VICTORY_PORT_CONSTANTS=1`, on here) has the card read certain draw arguments in place, in the game's memory.
- Batched or windowed preparation clamped these earlier than the game's visibility (culling) pass would have written them. So the draw got an earlier frame's records, or nothing.
- **The author's measurements:**
  - in the boat mission's cutscene, with a moving camera, palms and trees were missing for several frames (pop-5, pop-11…13);
  - not without ports 1 and 5 (pop-10);
  - with one-at-a-time preparation the palms stayed in every frame (pop-14);
  - **reopening the batch after the latest write was not enough (pop-15).** That matches our `INDIRECT_BATCH=1` result.
- **Its cost**, according to the README, is about 2–5 ms a frame.
