**Interpretation:**
- At the robbery neither the GPU nor our own preparing thread is slow: the card is idle 90% of the time.
- The game waits about 250 ms a frame at the present, and sends hardly any work meanwhile.
- Memory was at the limit for most of the run: victory used about 39 GB of private memory with 32 GB of RAM, free RAM around 1 GB, heavy page-ins.
- But the steady frame time of about 275 ms points more to a recurring wait than to random paging. The exact cause is not found yet.
- The bundle's author recommends 64 GB of RAM. KLBF stayed at about 25 GB in the same stretch, with its 512 px texture cap.

## The likely cause of the worse picture

- In Series S mode the bundle runs the game's own console FSR4 through shader translation. In the log: `FFX FSR4 ACO: DeserializeComputePipelineStateX FAILED … falling back to NewBE`, for every FSR4 pass.
- In the same profile KLBF uses AMD's PC FSR (`signedbin`). The bundle's PC FSR bridge only works in Series X 4K mode (`-Fsr4K`).
- The driver's compiler crashed 32 times while making pipelines (18:35–18:40). Those pipelines were left out: about 480 draws a frame in a normal scene, 2,850 at the robbery. That may also worsen the picture.

## Suggested next steps

1. **One run with option `2` (`-Safe`, without renderer ports) to the robbery.** If it does not drop to 3 fps there, a port is the cause.
2. **Porting KLBF's 512 px texture cap (`VICTORY_TEXTURE_MAX_DIM`, KLBF #63) into the bundle** to lower memory. The bundle's texture code differs from KLBF's, so this is a manual port.
3. **PC FSR in Series S** for the picture, based on KLBF's solution.
