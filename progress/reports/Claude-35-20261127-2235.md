# Claude-35 – A long-play measuring mode: memory, hitches, low FPS and F8 in one run

Date: 2026-11-27, 22:00–22:35 (local time). Tree: `VIctory-dev\belobug-9070` (uncommitted, base cd8dfc06). Follows Claude-34.

The user asked for one mode that watches everything while they play, so that a single long run gives the data on four things:
- the remaining memory growth;
- the hitches;
- the scenes that do not reach even 30 FPS (RT and a higher resolution would make them far worse);
- the late models.

They also asked to be told, each time, which option to play with.

## 1. The mode: `meresek\Inditas-Csomag.cmd`, option 0 (`meres-csomag.ps1 -Figyelo`)

It is option 1 (the bundle's default) plus the following, with no time limit:

- **Timeline recordings of 6 frames** (`timeline/record.rs`), past second 180. Three things begin one:
  - **A hitch:** a frame that came 250 ms or more after the one before (`VICTORY_TIMELINE_SLOW=250`, as in Claude-33).
  - **A slow scene (new):** the last 30 frames averaged 50 ms or more (`VICTORY_TIMELINE_LOW=50`, under 20 FPS).
  - **An F8 press (new):** the game looks at `jelolesek.csv` at most four times a second, and a recording begins when the file grows (`VICTORY_TIMELINE_MARKS`). The marker now runs for up to 4 hours in this mode (it was 1 hour).
- **Limits on the recordings:**
  - At least 90 s between automatic recordings (`VICTORY_TIMELINE_GAP=90`); a mark does not wait.
  - At most 60 recordings (`180x60`, a new spec form: n recordings from s on).
  - The 15 MB trace only for the first 4 (`VICTORY_TIMELINE_JSON_MOST=4`); every recording gets its 70–80 KB summary.
  - Files are `idovonal.<second>-<hitch|low|mark>.txt`.
- **Where our memory still grows (new, `VICTORY_HEAP_SITES=1`, `copystat.rs` `sites`).** The counting allocator samples one block in 1,024, chosen by its address hash so a release needs no lookup unless sampled. It keeps the block's 10 return addresses in fixed tables (no allocation in the allocator): 65,536 live blocks, 16,384 places, with idle places taken over. Once a minute the log has the 40 places that hold the most sampled bytes, as offsets in `victory.exe`.
- **The streaming log** (`betoltes.tsv`, as `-BetoltesNaplo`): every request's times, and each second's mapping costs. About 40 MB per 6 minutes, so about 280 MB for 40 minutes.
- **Analysis:** `python -I meresek\eszkozok\hosszu-elemzes.py <run folder>` (new, durable). It prints:
  1. memory per 5 minutes and per 10-minute slope, our own allocations, and the places that grew. These are symbolised with dbghelp and the PDB, and grouped by our first two functions.
  2. hitches per 5 minutes and the causes in the hitch recordings.
  3. slow 5-second windows by what bounded them, from the card-clock line: the card busy, the card idle behind our submitting thread, or idle with nothing submitted. Also the causes in the slow-scene recordings.
  4. each F8 mark with the window around it and its recording.
  5. streaming latency and mapping costs per minute.

## 3. What the short checks already show (not conclusions yet)

**Slow scenes.** Windows of over 40 ms a frame, from minute 3, using the card-clock line:

| Run | Card busy | Card idle behind our submitting thread | Card idle with nothing submitted |
|---|---|---|---|
| `212924-nezet-javitas` | 28.5 ms | 12.9 ms | 11.0 ms |
| `221013-hosszu-proba` | 23.3 ms | 13.1 ms | 7.6 ms |

- The card is busy only about half of each frame.
- The slow-scene recording (`idovonal.350-low.txt`), in frames of about 50 ms:
  - about 25 ms is the card running submissions;
  - most of the rest is our own chain: a job for another thread 4–6 ms, read-backs and telling the title about 3 ms, work spread over threads 2–3 ms, the submission's steps about 2 ms, threads woken but not running 1–3 ms.
- At a higher resolution or with RT, the card's part grows. Ours does not shrink by itself.

**Memory places that grew in the first 5 minutes** (estimates from samples; the long run will say which keep growing):
- shader translation (`shader_cache::decode`, `pipeline::shader`): bounded by the shaders;
- the diagnostics map `descriptor::host::note_before` (`BEFORE`, per slot);
- `book::note` (the notes of the book's entries, up to 600 bytes each);
- placed-texture resources: `resource::create_at_address` and `hand_out_resource`, with `resource::hosted`. These are kept for the run by design (`PLACED_TEXTURES`). Only their host textures are let go.

The last item is the likeliest source of the ~0.1 GB/min left after Claude-34: every texture the title streams to a new address adds a resource object with its book entry.
