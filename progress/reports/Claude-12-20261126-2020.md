## The picture: PC AMD FSR in Series S

- **Cause (Claude-10):** in Series S the bundle runs the game's console FSR4 by translating it (`falling back to NewBE`).
  - The bundle's PC FSR bridge (`victory-hle/src/modules/fidelityfx.rs`, DexNom's port) only turned on with the Series X 4K profile (`-Fsr4K`) until now.
  - KLBF does the same in Series S with `VICTORY_FSR_DLL`.
- **Change** (`apps/victory/src/run.rs`, 12 lines):
  - if a FidelityFX folder is given (`VICTORY_FIDELITYFX_DIR`), the bridge serves the game's FSR calls in every profile;
  - `VICTORY_FSR_PC=0` turns it off.
- **Test start** (75 s, before the account picker):
  - `FidelityFX: selected PC upscaler … version="4.1.2"`;
  - `upscaler context on the host … render=[1280, 720] output=[1920, 1080]`;
  - `FidelityFX 4 recorded the title's temporal dispatch`;
  - no panic or error.
- The in-game picture has not been seen yet.

## Flicker and pop-in

- The bundle's README lists as a known fault: "Trees, signs and far objects flicker in the distance in some places", and textures that just came into view arrive late.
- The skipped compute pipelines (100–480 a frame, `DROP` on the HUD) are mostly the same as on KLBF's AMD ban list (`skip-amd.txt`), e.g. `a4df980a…`, `a73284b6…`, `d2c52fcd…`. So this is no difference from KLBF.
- `VICTORY_TEXTURE_NO_WAIT=1` (the default in the bundle's `play.ps1`) may contribute to the pop-in: draws do not wait for the texture. I did not change it.
