## 3. The character ray-tracing resolve: blue faces and glowing eyes

**The video's marks:**

| Video | Phase | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 51.9 s | LEARN on | the counsellor's face and neck are blue-purple |
| 65.2 s | LEARN off | the same |
| 94.0 s | LEARN on | Lucia's eyes glow white-blue, her teeth are dark |

**The contact sheet** (every 1.5 s): the counsellor's face is blue in every setting (40.5–93 s). In the 118–122 s beach montage the pedestrians' skin has greenish-grey patches.

**Cause:**
- Since Claude-23 the 41 `ped_*:CS_RaytracingResolve` shaders run (Belobug `f962387` + avatarmanz #430).
- But the ray-tracing acceleration structure is turned off (`dropped … ray-tracing structure builder, which is off`), so rays hit nothing. The resolve writes the sky's colour (blue) onto skin, eyes and glasses.
- **Not `LEARN`:** the fault shows in both on and off phases.
- **It was not there in the 09:33 run:** those shaders were skipped then, and only the glasses looked odd.

**Fix:**
- `meres-csomag.ps1 -RtFeloldas ki`, which is the default;
- the 42 shader identifiers are listed in `csomag\rt-feloldo-shaderek.txt`;
- the existing `VICTORY_SKIP_PIPELINES` switch skips them.

This brings the picture back to the morning's state.

**Check:** the test start (`skip-a`, 135 s, with F9 recording) waited at the account picker (`ACCOUNT PICKER (Y) / PLAYER (A)`), which needs a button press. So **the user has to look at the scene**.

**The next step towards real ray tracing**, if the resolve has to run:
- either rays get a clean "no hit" answer without the acceleration structure;
- or the reflection is clamped to the room's light (maren-halv `Role::Reflect`; fxceless `4966cd67` / Op-lite `183ac80f`).

These can come behind their own switches, after checking them first.
