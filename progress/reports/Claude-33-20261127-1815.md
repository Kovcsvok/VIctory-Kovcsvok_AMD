# Claude-33 – The memory jump at the world load, frame hitches (pipeline waits), and the late high-detail models

Date: 2026-11-27, 17:30–18:15 (local time). Tree: `VIctory-dev\belobug-9070` (uncommitted, base cd8dfc06). Follows Claude-32. The user asked, in order: what the memory jump at the first world load is, then the frame-time spikes, then the late-loading crude models (not always in the same place; sometimes only the motorway loads late).

## 1. The memory jump at the first world load (from the logs of Claude-32's runs)

New script `memsplit32.py` (scratchpad) splits the 5-second memory lines. In run 2 (17:14), between 16:16:09 and 16:16:25 UTC:

| | 16:16:09 | 16:16:25 |
|---|---|---|
| Private | 20.25 GB | 24.91 GB |
| Host textures | 47,554 (5.48 GB) | 73,943 (7.94 GB) |
| Card memory in use | 7.9 GB | 10.4 GB |
| Shared memory in use | 5.9 GB | 7.3 GB |
| Title-memory host copies | 3.68 GB | 4.60 GB (windows 3.06 → 3.97 GB) |

- The title streams in about 30,000 textures in 15 s.
- Most of the private growth is the title's own memory filling, which we do not hold. Our part is the window copies (+0.9 GB) and staging.
- Run 1 (17:04) had the lower peak because it had loaded fewer textures by then (62–65 k, not 73 k), not because of the lists.
- Seen on the way: host copies of placed textures whose title memory is not mapped any more grew from 1 to 3.7 GB (estimate, `resource::census`). They are on the card (budget 15.4 GB, 10.9 GB in use), kept by the sweep's 10-minute rule below 85 % (`host::vram_keep`). They are not in the PC's RAM, so they do not count for the 18 GiB working-set goal.
- **What is ours to cut at the peak: the windows' host copies (2.6–4.0 GB).** No change here.

## 2. Measuring hitches (new)

- `copystat.rs`: a line every ~5 s, `frame gaps since the last line: N over 50 ms, N over 100 ms, N over 250 ms; the longest … ms`. Each gap of 250 ms or more is logged with its time (`a frame came N ms after the one before`, the first 300).
- `timeline/record.rs`: `VICTORY_TIMELINE_SLOW=<ms>`. The timeline waits, past its time, for a frame that came that long after the one before, and records the frames after it. Launcher: `-Idovonal '<s>[+<s>..],<frames>' -IdovonalLassu <ms>`, files `idovonal.<s>.json/.txt` in the run folder.
- Script `hitch32.py` (scratchpad): hitches per 5-s window against streaming MB/s, texture uploads, preparing, page faults, write-watch, new pipelines.

## 3. What the hitches are

- **Run `20261127-173330-betoltes-ab` (10 min):**
  - 1.0 % of frames over 100 ms, 23 of 250 ms or more;
  - most in the first 1.5 min after the world loads;
  - a run of 8 frames of ~480 ms at 16:41:37 UTC, beginning with new shader translations.
  - Weak correlation with every logged signal (page faults +0.31 the strongest).
- **Timeline of run `20261127-174604-akadas-idovonal`, third recording:**
  - a 508 ms frame: 243 ms on `victory-pipelines-8`, and 258 ms a wait that ended on it;
  - a 243 ms frame: 240 ms on `victory-pipelines-0`.
  - The present's chain stood at a pipeline worker, outside our spans, in the driver's compile.
  - Another recording, a 103 ms frame: 59 ms in the title's `CopyPageMappings` (xmem).
- **Cause, `pipeline/make.rs::wait`:** a first use of a pipeline not made yet waits within a budget of 500 ms per title frame (`VICTORY_PIPELINE_WAIT_MS`; passes over the screen 10 s). A pipeline that takes seconds to make cost **every** frame its whole budget, eight in a row. Each frame's draw was dropped anyway once its budget was spent.

## 4. Fix: a pipeline is waited for in one frame only (`VICTORY_PIPELINE_WAIT_ONCE=1`)

- `Pending::waited_in` holds the first title frame in which a first use waited with budget left. Later frames do not wait for it again: its draws are dropped until it is made, as they were after each wait.
- A pass the title runs once still gets its wait, in its frame. A first use in a frame whose budget is already spent is not marked, so it gets its wait in a later frame, as before.
- Counted in the gap line (`first uses of pipelines not waited for again`). Changes live with `VICTORY_AB`.
- Tests: the pipeline and timeline tests (32) pass.
- Launcher: `-ShaderVaras egyszer|mindig`, **default `egyszer` since 18:10**.

Runs, gameplay only (from 150 s after start), 10 min each, no device error:

| Run | Wait | Frames | Over 100 ms | 250 ms or more | Pipelines made new | Not waited again |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 17:33 `betoltes-ab` | every frame | 10,242 | 38 (0.37 %) | 10 | 19 | – |
| 17:46 `akadas-idovonal` | every frame | 10,372 | 27 (0.26 %) | 14 | 83 | – |
| 17:59 `shader-egyszer` | **one frame** | 10,461 | 26 (0.25 %) | **2** | 17 | 7 |

- With about as many new pipelines as the 17:33 run (17 against 19), the hitches of 250 ms or more after the load fell from 10 to 2.
- In the window where the rule acted 7 times, no frame took over 100 ms.
- One run each, and the user's play differs, so this is a direction.

## 5. The late high-detail models (streaming)

- `dstorage.rs`: the 5-s stats line is now at info, with `staging_mb`. `VICTORY_DSTORAGE_STAGING_WIDE` raises the staging limit to at least 256 MiB, as codec-d/cool_fox 641835a4 does, and changes live.
- Live A/B every 30 s in the 17:33 run: **no staging waits in either phase**, at most 4.3 of 20 workers busy, 0–850 requests waiting only at the moment a batch was submitted. Up to 606 MB/s at the world load, 12–160 MB/s in play.
- **Our streaming is not behind: what the title asks for is read at once.** A late model is asked for late by the title, or its data waits elsewhere.
- One candidate seen in the timeline: the title's page-mapping calls (`CopyPageMappings` 59 ms in one frame, `XMemMapPhysicalPages` 4.9 ms in another). The title maps the pages of every streaming batch before it submits it. In the bundle they run one thread at a time (`VICTORY_MAP_THREADS=1`).
- Prepared, not run: `meres-csomag.ps1 -BetoltesNaplo` writes `VICTORY_DSTORAGE_LOG` to the run folder. It gives every request's enqueue, submit, start and done times, and each second's mapping costs. A live A/B of `VICTORY_MAP_THREADS` (1 against 8, `-AB '30,VICTORY_MAP_THREADS'`) with it would show whether mapping holds the title's requests back.
