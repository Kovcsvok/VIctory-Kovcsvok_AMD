## Is Codex's description right? By the code, yes.

`crates/victory-gpu/src/draw.rs`, `batches()` and `State::buffers_written()`:

- **Mode 1 (the code's default):** every recorded shader that may write a buffer closes the open batch. So the arguments of the next indirect execution are clamped again, after the work recorded before them.
- **Mode 2 (`play.ps1` and the current base profile):** a dispatch only marks the batch. It stays open until the game's next barrier, pipeline sync or buffer-writing draw.
- **The risk of mode 2, in the code's own comment:** "whether the title has ways to wait that do not pass through its lists' methods is not known". If the game waits that way, a step of the visibility chain may read old arguments, and then the whole object may be left out. Codex's hypothesis rests exactly on this.
- **Expected cost:** on the author's replayed frame mode 2 took 75.3 ms in 133 passes, denser batching 77.7 ms in 980 passes. That is about +2.4 ms a frame (3%). Under the 30 fps cap it probably does not show.
