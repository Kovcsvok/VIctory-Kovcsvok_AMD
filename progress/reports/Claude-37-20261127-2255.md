# Claude-37 – The long run froze at 12.4 min: the heap-places report waited for itself (fixed)

Date: 2026-11-27, 22:45–22:55 (local time). Tree: `VIctory-dev\belobug-9070` (uncommitted, base cd8dfc06). Follows Claude-35 and Claude-36.

## 1. What happened

The user played option 0 (`20261127-223226-hosszu-meres`). At 22:44:53 the game stopped and stayed stopped. At 22:46 the process was still alive but not responding: 150 threads, 0.05 s of CPU in 2 s, 25.15 GB private. The user's F8 at 22:45:03 came after the stop.

**Cause: Claude-35's own diagnostics (`VICTORY_HEAP_SITES`), not the title and not the GPU.**
- The memory log writes its lines in a fixed order. In every earlier minute, "shapes of placed views" is followed by "where our blocks were made (VICTORY_HEAP_SITES)", for example at 21:43:51.407738 and 21:43:51.408000.
- The last lines of the logging thread, at 21:44:52.990858, are "shapes of placed views" and then nothing. This was its 13th minute, the minute that line is due. After that only one line from another thread follows (21:44:53.000783), and the log ends.
- `heap_sites()` held `sites::TABLE` while it collected the places into a new `Vec`. `collect()` from a filter grows the vector step by step, and every step allocates and frees a block.
- One block in 1,024 is sampled by its address. A sampled block calls `sites::made` or `sites::gone`, which lock the same `TABLE`.
- So the reporting thread, once it hit a sampled block, waited for the lock it held: Rust's `Mutex` is not reentrant. From then on, every thread that made or freed a sampled block, about one allocation in 1,024, waited behind it, and the game stopped within moments.
- The chance per minute is small: a few allocations, each with a 1-in-1,024 chance. Over 12 reports it came up once. The two short checks in Claude-35 (6 and 5 min) did not hit it.

## 2. The fix (`crates/victory-gpu/src/copystat.rs`)

- `heap_sites()` makes room for all 16,384 places (`Vec::with_capacity(sites::SITES)`, 1.5 MB) **before** it takes the lock. Under the lock it only copies into that room, so nothing allocates there.
- `sites::reporting(f)` holds the table for the report and sets a thread-local flag (`REPORTING`, a `const` `Cell<bool>`, which allocates nothing). While the flag is set, `made` and `gone` on that thread return at once instead of waiting. If something under the lock ever allocates again, the block goes uncounted and nothing hangs.
- New test `sites::tests::the_report_does_not_wait_for_itself`: it makes and frees a block inside `reporting` (the test would hang without the guard), and checks that the same block made outside the report is counted.
