## Launcher

- `Inditas-Csomag.cmd`, which calls `meresek\eszkozok\meres-csomag.ps1`.
  - `1`: normal. The bundle's `scripts\play.ps1` settings, plus:
    - all five renderer ports (`VICTORY_PORT_CONSTANTS`, `_DESCRIPTORS`, `_CBV`, `_GUARD`, `_JOURNAL`);
    - `--console series-s`;
    - `VICTORY_PANEL=1` (fps display in the picture).
  - `2`: the bundle's `-Safe` mode, without ports.
- It measures like the KLBF launcher: `victory.log`, `memoria.csv`, F8 marks, a slice of `crashes.log`, `futas.txt`.
- **Differences from the KLBF measurements:**
  - no 512 px texture cap, because the bundle has no `VICTORY_TEXTURE_MAX_DIM`;
  - no `frametime.csv`; the fps lines are in `victory.log` (`N fps, M ms/frame`);
  - according to the bundle's author it uses about 30 GB of private memory (64 GB of RAM recommended). This machine has 32 GB of RAM and a 40 GiB page file; the commit limit is 71.85 GiB.
